# Terms of Service: Outline for Counsel

> This is an outline, not legal text. **Counsel must draft and approve the final Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before launch.**

1. **Eligibility.** US citizens aged 18 or older. One account per person, ever.
2. **Verification.** Verification is done through a third party (SheerID). Demanity stores only a one-way uniqueness token, which it keeps even after the account is deleted. This is how the one-account rule is enforced.
3. **Account security.** At least one 2FA method is required (email or phone). Passkeys are used for voting. Account recovery requires re-verification.
4. **Accounts are personal.** **No transferring, selling, renting or sharing accounts.**
5. **Prohibited conduct.**
   - everything listed in [ABUSE.md](ABUSE.md)
   - coordinated inauthentic behavior and paid campaigns to influence opinion
   - misinformation
   - hate based on race, gender or religion
   - harassment
   - manipulating polls
6. **Enforcement.**
   - **Any abuse, in any way, shape or form, may result in a permanent ban.** Because of the uniqueness token, a banned person cannot create a new account.
   - **Anyone who buys accounts or tries to sway public opinion may face legal action in addition to a ban.**
7. **Content and citations.**
   - Factual claims must cite approved sources.
   - Moderators may add context notes or remove content.
   - Every moderation decision can be appealed.
8. **Leader pages.**
   - Leader pages are built from public records.
   - Leaders who claim their page cannot edit their trust page or cite themselves.
9. **Privacy.** A summary of [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md):
   - Demanity never sells user data.
   - Ballots cannot be linked to the person who cast them.
   - Public posts can still identify the person who wrote them.
10. **Polls.** Results reflect self-selected users and are not representative of the US population.
11. **Ads.** Ads are contextual only and never tailored to user data.
12. **Open source.** The code is licensed under AGPL. The Demanity trademark is covered by [TRADEMARK.md](../TRADEMARK.md).
13. **Disputes, liability and changes to these terms.** Counsel to draft.
