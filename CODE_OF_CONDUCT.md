## Additions specific to Demanity
- **Political views.** Contributors hold every political view. Debate ideas, never people.
- **No partisan campaigning** in project spaces.
- **Project contributors only.** This code covers people working on the project. People using the platform are covered by the platform's Terms of Service and [docs/MODERATION.md](docs/MODERATION.md).
