# Trademark Policy (Draft, for counsel review)

"Demanity" and the Demanity logo identify the official platform run by the Demanity organization.

**Allowed:**
- forking and running the code under the AGPL;
- saying your fork is "based on Demanity".

**Not allowed:** calling your fork or instance "Demanity", or using the logo, in a way that suggests it is official or endorsed.

**Why:** an impostor "official" instance could alter polls or scores and mislead the public.
