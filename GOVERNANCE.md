# Governance

## Structure
- **Organization:** a 501(c)(4) nonprofit (incorporation pending). It owns the trademark and operates the official instance.
- **Maintainers:** review and merge code and keep the roadmap.
- **Moderation team:** the founder assembles a real-world team with final authority over verification and moderation ([docs/MODERATION.md](docs/MODERATION.md)).
- **Advisory board:** a cross-partisan board ([docs/NONPARTISANSHIP.md](docs/NONPARTISANSHIP.md)).

## Accountability
- **Organization leader page.** Users vote on platform decisions. Votes are advisory, and the organization must respond publicly to any vote above the participation threshold.
- **Public logs.** Moderation actions are recorded in an append-only public log.
- **Transparency reports.** Published quarterly.
- **Changes to scoring, taxonomy or sources.** A 30-day comment period, then cross-partisan sign-off.

## Decision-making
- **Code:** lazy consensus among maintainers. Disputes go to a maintainer vote.
- **Platform policy:** decided by the organization, after advisory-board review and a public response to user votes.
