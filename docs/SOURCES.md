# Approved Citation Sources (R33)

> ## ⚠️ REVIEW BEFORE RELEASE
> This is a draft list. Changes follow the cross-partisan process in [NONPARTISANSHIP.md §6](NONPARTISANSHIP.md#6-changes-to-scoring-taxonomy-and-sources).

## Rule
**Every factual claim on Demanity must cite at least one source from this list.** The rule covers:
- leader citations
- moderator context notes
- fact-check outcomes
- contributor notes on legislation
- devil's-advocate entries

Opinions do not need a citation, but they must be presented as opinion.

The app checks every link against this list when it is submitted. A link is accepted when its host is a listed domain or a subdomain of one. The whole `.gov` domain is listed as Tier 1, because only U.S. government bodies can register `.gov` names. Domains not on the list are rejected, and the submitter can request that the domain be added.

## Tier 1: Primary official sources (source-quality weight 1.0)
- **Federal legislation and records:**
  - [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/)
  - [GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/)
  - [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/)
  - [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/)
  - [Senate](https://www.senate.gov/) and [House Clerk](https://clerk.house.gov/) roll calls
- **Executive and statistical agencies:**
  - [WhiteHouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/)
  - [Census](https://www.census.gov/)
  - [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/)
  - [FEC](https://www.fec.gov/)
  - [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/)
  - [FBI Crime Data Explorer](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/)
  - [CBO](https://www.cbo.gov/)
  - [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/)
- **Courts:**
  - [US Courts](https://www.uscourts.gov/)
  - [Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/)
  - [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/), which republishes court records
  - PACER
- **States:**
  - official state legislature, governor and court websites (`.gov` or the official state domain)
  - [Open States](https://openstates.org/), which republishes state legislative records
- **Leaders' own words:** a leader's own official website, press releases and verified official social media accounts. These are allowed as evidence of what a leader *said*, not as evidence that what they said is true.

## Tier 2: Wire services and fact-checkers (weight 0.9)
- **Wire services:** [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/) and [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/).
- **Fact-checkers:** verified signatories of the [International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) Code of Principles](https://www.ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/).
- **C-SPAN:** [C-SPAN](https://www.c-span.org/) video archives, for primary footage.

## Tier 3: Established news outlets (weight 0.8)
**Admission criteria.** An outlet is admitted only if it meets **all** of these. The same criteria apply to outlets of every political lean:
1. A public corrections policy, and a record of actually issuing corrections.
2. Named editorial staff and a physical newsroom.
3. A clear separation of news content from opinion content. Only news content may be cited.
4. No record of repeated fabrication.

**Balance.** The initial list must include right-, center- and left-leaning outlets. The cross-partisan review board approves the list. Outlets are **not** named here until that review takes place.

## Not accepted
Anonymous blogs, unverified social media posts, AI-generated content without a primary source, content farms, and partisan advocacy material presented as fact.

## Reversals
Any fact-check outcome can be reversed by a **different** moderator panel, with no overlap with the original panel. Reversals are logged publicly ([MODERATION.md](MODERATION.md)).
