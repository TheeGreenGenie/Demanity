# Bipartisan Governance Plan (R28)

How people perceive Demanity decides whether it survives. Being nonpartisan has to be **built into the organization's structure, and checkable by anyone**, not just promised.

## 1. Organizational neutrality
- **No endorsements.** Demanity never endorses a candidate or a party.
- **Only official positions.** Its only official positions concern the platform itself: privacy, transparency and access to public records.
- **Within IRS limits.** It stays within the 501(c)(4) limits on political campaign activity ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/other-non-profits/social-welfare-organizations)).

## 2. Funding
- **No party or campaign money.** Demanity refuses money from parties, campaigns and candidate committees.
- **Donor caps.** Large donors are disclosed publicly beyond what the law requires.
- **No donor influence.** Donors have no say over content, scoring or moderation.

## 3. Moderation team
- **Recruitment.** Moderators are recruited across the political spectrum.
- **Recorded lean.** Each moderator's self-declared political lean is recorded internally. Only the aggregate is published.
- **Mixed panels.** Every fact-check panel of three has a mix of leanings.
- **Training.** Moderators train on the acceptable-use policy ([ABUSE.md](ABUSE.md)), the team's private abuse playbook, and the neutral-language style guide.

## 4. Symmetric-enforcement audits
- **What is counted.** Every quarter, moderation actions are tallied by the party of the leader involved and by the political lean of the content: takedowns, context notes, fact-check outcomes, citation approvals and verification overrides.
- **Who reviews it.** An external auditor reviews the figures when funding allows.
- **Publication.** Results are published, along with an explanation for any imbalance.

## 5. Advisory board
- **Membership.** The board has balanced representation: Democrats, Republicans and independents.
- **Responsibilities.** It reviews:
  - changes to scoring and taxonomy;
  - changes to the source list;
  - audits;
  - organization-page votes that crossed the participation threshold.

## 6. Changes to scoring, taxonomy and sources
1. Publish the proposal, with the reasoning behind it and the expected effects.
2. Hold a **30-day public comment period.**
3. Obtain **cross-partisan sign-off** from the advisory board.
4. Record the change in the public changelog. It takes effect after a set delay.

## 7. Staff conduct
- **No partisan activity in Demanity's name.** Staff may not campaign or post partisan content under the Demanity name or in a Demanity role.
- **Neutral language.** Staff follow the **neutral-language style guide**. Where the two sides use different terms for the same thing, both terms are shown.

## 8. Data uniformity
- **Same rules for everyone.** Every leader page is generated from official data using the same rules.
- **Same tools for everyone.** No leader gets special features. Every leader who claims a page can use the same tools.

## 9. Red teams
Red-team exercises run regularly, with participants from across the spectrum who try to make the platform look biased. Their findings are published along with the fixes.

## 10. Organization leader page
- **Advisory votes.** Users vote on platform decisions. The votes are advisory, but Demanity **must publicly respond** to any vote above the participation threshold.
- **Context notes.** Moderators may add context notes to counter misinformation.
- **Categories.** The page will gain accountability categories once success measures are defined.
