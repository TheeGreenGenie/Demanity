# Acceptable Use and Enforcement (R27)

Demanity exists so that one verified person has one honest voice. This page explains what counts as abuse of the platform and what happens to people who abuse it.

> The moderation and technology teams keep detailed detection and prevention methods privately, so abusers can't use them to evade enforcement. Vetted contributors can request access (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#sensitive-documents)).

## Prohibited conduct
1. **Identity fraud.** This includes using fake, stolen or someone else's identity documents, and trying to create more than one account.
2. **Account trading.** Buying, selling, renting, sharing or transferring a verified account.
3. **Poll manipulation.** This includes:
   - automated voting or voting on someone else's behalf;
   - pressuring other people to vote a certain way;
   - attempting to tamper with results or de-anonymize voters.
4. **Coordinated inauthentic behavior.** This includes:
   - paid or organized campaigns that pretend to be independent voices;
   - foreign influence operations;
   - any attempt to sway public opinion through fake or coordinated accounts.
5. **Citation abuse.**
   - Knowingly false or out-of-context citations.
   - Trivial citations submitted to game scores.
   - Self-citation by leaders. The platform blocks this.
6. **Contributor misconduct.**
   - Plagiarized or ghostwritten applications.
   - Leaking or doxxing from contributor chats.
   - Fake legislation submissions.
7. **Moderation abuse.** By moderators: biased decisions, misusing overrides, collusion. By anyone: trying to manipulate the fact-check process, including the AI review.
8. **Harassment and hate.**
   - Hate based on race, gender or religion.
   - Harassment, doxxing and threats.
9. **Platform attacks.**
   - Scraping to identify users.
   - Attacking or overloading the service.
   - Probing for vulnerabilities outside our [security policy](../SECURITY.md).

## Consequences
- **Enforcement ladder.** Depending on severity: warning, then temporary restriction, then **permanent ban**, then **legal referral**. Severe cases can skip straight to a permanent ban or a legal referral.
- **Permanent bans last for good.** Each person can hold only one account, ever, so a banned person cannot sign up again.
- **Legal action.** **People who buy accounts or coordinate to sway public opinion may face legal action in addition to a ban.**
- **Insiders are not exempt.** Founders, staff and moderators are held to these rules too, and every moderation action is publicly logged ([MODERATION.md](MODERATION.md)).

## Reporting
- **Abuse:** report it from any piece of content, or report an account directly.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** report them privately under [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
